Numeric limits, constants, and mathematical functions: the integer and float
range bounds, the constants π, τ, e, infinity, and NaN, absolute value,
rounding and truncation, sign and comparison helpers, division remainders,
powers and roots, exponentials and logarithms, trigonometric and hyperbolic
functions, angle conversion, float classification, and integer helpers such
as `gcd`, `lcm`, and `factorial`.

**Examples**

```gust
use math

def main() -> none
    print(math.sqrt(2.0))             # 1.4142135623730951
    print(math.gcd(24, 36))           # 12
    print(math.clamp(5.0, 0.0, 1.0))  # 1.0
end
```
